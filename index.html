THE VENUE

OUR VENUE

Our Venue

Located in the heart of Auckland’s CBD, The Mothership is an independent venue and progressive platform rooted in underground culture.

Forward-thinking, detail-driven and artist-aligned, we offer a constantly evolving space that reimagines live music, atmosphere and experience with every event.

Designed for collaboration, made to adapt and purpose-built for every sound and scene. Whether hosting international acts, live performances, tailored corporate activations or private events, the venue moves easily between layouts, giving each event the freedom to take on its own identity.

Built for sound, performance & community.
